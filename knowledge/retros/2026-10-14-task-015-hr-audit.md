# Retro — TASK-015 HR audit report (2026-10-14)

Scope: `GET /api/audit/:userId`, the `/audit` page (employee picker, optional UTC date range, include-soft-deleted
toggle, per-request event timeline), `auditQuerySchema`, the Audit log nav item, and a reconciliation of four documents
that disagreed about soft-deleted rows. Verified with the full gate (171 tests and a production build listing
`/api/audit/[userId]` and `/audit`) and a real Postgres 18: 12 checks through the real route and SQL, run twice, harness
deleted, throwaway database dropped. No product defect found. Two harness mistakes (non-unique test ids and emails) were
fixed along the way.

## Decisions taken (confirmed by approving the plan)

- The default report **excludes soft-deleted requests** (CSV AC2, architecture doc). `include_deleted=true` shows them,
  flagged "Deleted", with their full timeline; the row is never purged and a hard delete is refused by the database.
- Optional `from`/`to` (UTC calendar days, inclusive). A request matches when **any** of its audit events falls in the
  range, and its full timeline is still shown.
- The employee's free-text `reason` is **never selected** (it can hold medical details); the rejection note is included.
- Unknown employee id → 404; an employee with no requests → 200 and an empty list. No time cutoff and no row cap.
- HR admin only, deliberately **not** limited to direct reports ("any employee ID"): the role gate is the control.

## Verified on a real database

- AC1 from genuine submit, approve and reject flows: newest first, decision-maker name, decision time, rejection note and
  the event timeline; another team's employee (Leo) is viewable by HR; manager and employee sessions get 403, no session 401.
- AC2: excluded by default, shown flagged on request, still in the table, hard delete refused, row counts unchanged.
- A request and events from 4 years ago are returned with no range (no hidden cutoff).
- Date boundaries hold to the second (23:59:59Z vs 00:00:00Z) under four database time zones.
- Read-only: row counts and the balance total are unchanged however often the report runs.
- Scale: 3,000 requests (6,000 events) for one employee returned in full in about 140 ms.
