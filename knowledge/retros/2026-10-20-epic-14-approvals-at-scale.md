# Retro: EPIC-14, Team Approvals and Balance Overrides at scale (TASK-075 to 078)

Built and validated locally, **not committed, not pushed**. No migration, no new route, no role or required-feature change; nothing under `docs/` except the new `docs/dev-tasks/epic-14-approvals-at-scale-tasks.csv` (status `Open`). Decided with the user on 2026-10-20: history as a second tab on each page, the filters below, a table, no bulk approve, both pages in one go.

## Why

Team Approvals loaded every pending request of a manager's team with no filter, no paging and no search; Balance Overrides stopped at the oldest 50 ("oldest 50 of N"), so with more waiting HR could not reach the rest at all (a functional gap, not only polish). Both "Recently decided" and "Recently overridden" were a hard-coded last 10.

## What shipped

- **Shared UI** (extracted from Leave Policies, which now uses it unchanged): `ui/pager`, `ui/stat-tiles`, `ui/list-filters` (select, filter form, search row, phone-collapsible selects, chips), `ui/view-tabs`, `ui/sort-header`.
- **Address parsing** (`list-query.ts`, `team-queue-schema.ts`, `override-list-schema.ts`): `view`, `q`, `type`, `status` (manager) or `team` (HR), `from`, `to`, `sort`, `dir`, `per` (25, 50, 100), `page`. A bad value is ignored and named in a notice; a status or sort of the other view is ignored; sort is a fixed list per view and never SQL text.
- **Reads** (`team-lists.ts`, `override-lists.ts`; one statement each, scope and role checked in the SQL): `getTeamQueuePage` (a manager's pending requests), `getTeamDecidedPage`, `getTeamChoices`; `getOverrideQueuePage` (every request waiting for HR), `getOverriddenPage` (from the audit log), `getOverrideChoices`. Filters and page size are bound values; the order is a `CASE` over validated values carried as a row number through the page; the total is counted before the cut and the page clamped in SQL; a summary for the tiles ignores the filters.
  - The manager's **balance state is computed in SQL** (`ok`, `over`, `blocked`) so it can filter, sort and page, and mirrors `checkOverBalance` (a gated test compares the two over 11 cases, including exactly enough, 0.01 short, no record, negative, untracked and weekend-only).
  - HR's waiting list keeps **exactly** the conditions of the old queue, `getOverrideCount` and `overrideLeaveRequest`; unfiltered its total equals the dashboard's count (gated, read in one snapshot).
  - The old `getTeamQueue`, `getRecentlyDecided`, `getOverrideQueue` and `getRecentOverrides` stay: the API route `GET /api/leave-requests?scope=team` and the gated parity tests still use them.
- **Pages:** tabs (Pending and Decided; Waiting and Overridden), three tiles, a search and filter card (name, leave type, status or team, leave dates, sort, rows per page, chips), a table from `xl` (sticky header, sort links with `aria-sort`, the reason beneath the dates, the balance state in words, Approve and Reject or Approve override) and the original cards below, with the numbered pager. The decision buttons are the **same client component** in both structures, so the decision flows, the over-balance rule and the override rules are unchanged. Two statements per page view (the list with its tiles, and the filter choices).

## Found and fixed during validation

- **[FIX NOW, mutation] Two weak assertions.** "The decided list needs the manager role check" only held if a non-manager had decided requests, and "the HR page is clamped in SQL" only checked a page number computed in TypeScript. A non-manager with a team and a decided request, and the clamped page's rows, are now asserted.
- **[FIX NOW, flaky gated test] Company-wide comparisons raced with other gated files.** `team-lists` inserts over-balance requests while `override-lists` compared the waiting total with a separate count. The comparisons and the summary checks now read ONE snapshot (`isolation level repeatable read read only`).
- **[FIX NOW, from the screenshots] "Approve override" wrapped to two lines (56 px) in a narrow actions column.** The actions cells have a minimum width; every button is 44 px on one line, and no table scrolls sideways at 1280, 1440 or 1728.
- Harness slips only: the name selector read the avatar's initials, a few waits ran before hydration, and a rerun on a used database changed counts.

## Not seen

## Backlog (nothing blocking)

- No bulk approve (decided with the user). Selecting many requests and approving them at once is the natural next step and needs the same care as bulk policy edits: a preview, one transaction, a cap and an audit row per request.
- The manager read scans `leave_requests` (8 ms at 20,000 rows); at about 10 times that, a partial index on `(status) where is_deleted = false` would need a migration.
- The HR waiting read is linear in the company's pending requests (about 30 ms at 8,000 waiting).
- The tile "can't be approved yet" has no link (it is two statuses, over and blocked).
- The date filter means "leave dates overlap this range"; the decided view has no filter on when it was decided.
- Another HR Admin's decision shows in an open page only after a reload or the next decision.
